Drawing a human being's head To draw the shape of a human being's head, consider the following steps: (i) Draw two overlapping circular shapes—one resembling a ball and the other shaped like a mango; (ii) Divide the shape you have drawn in to three parts using lines; (iii) Draw line to indicate the position of the ears, eyes, nose, and mouth; and (iv) Then, draw the ears, eyes, nose, and mouth following the guidelines you created in step three.
