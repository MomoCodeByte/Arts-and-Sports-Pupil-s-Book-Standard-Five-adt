(a) Catching and controlling the ball Catching and controlling the ball is an important skill in netball. The skill allows the team to maintain possession of the ball in a game. To achieve effective receiving and controlling the ball, the player should: (i) Keep the hands open and the fingers pointing forward. This allows the player to control the ball effectively. Fingers pointing forward help the player to create a shape for receiving the ball easily. (ii) Absorb the ball’s momentum by softening your hands on impact. When the ball lands in the hands, the player should slightly pull back the hands to reduce the impact of the collision and keep the ball safe. (iii) Use the eyes to track the ball to determine its direction: The player’s eyes should be on the ball at all times to see its direction and prepare to receive it correctly. Catching and controlling the ball is an important skill in netball. This skill helps the team keep the ball during a game. To receive and control the ball well, the player should: (i) Keep the hands open and the fingers pointing forward. This helps the player control the ball well. Fingers that point forward help the player make a good shape to receive the ball easily. (ii) Take in the ball's force by keeping your hands soft when the ball hits them. When the ball lands in the hands, the player should pull the hands back a little. This reduces the force of the hit and helps keep the ball safe. (iii) Use the eyes to follow the ball and see which way it is going: The player's eyes should stay on the ball all the time. This helps the player see its direction and get ready to receive it correctly. This also helps stop the ball from being lost.
