Activity 2

Using various online sources, investigate a song that involves more than one voice.

(a) Choose a song that involves more than one voice, then sing that song with your fellow, ensuring that your voice is neither too loud nor too soft.
(b) After singing, ask yourself: If your voice had a balance with the voices of your colleagues.
(c) Briefly write down your answers, explaining what helped you to achieve a good balance of voices.
