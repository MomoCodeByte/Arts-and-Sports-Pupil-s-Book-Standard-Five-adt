(d) Doing physical exercises like running and jumping: Physical exercises are important for every netball player as they increase stamina, speed, and strength. Importance of playing netball Netball is a fast-paced game that requires good cooperation between players to achieve victory. Apart from being a recreational game, netball provides many benefits to the individual player and society as a whole. The following are some of the important benefits of playing netball: (a) Improves physical health. Playing netball is one of the best ways to improve physical well-being. This game involves many movements such as running, jumping, and passing the ball, which increase muscle strength, speed, and physical endurance. It also helps to improve the heart and lungs, lose weight, and improve blood circulation, thus reducing the risk of non-communicable diseases such as blood pressure and diabetes. (b) Builds cooperation and friendship between players. Netball is a team game that requires good communication and cooperation between players. Through practice and matches, players learn to help each other, understand each other, and cooperate with the aim of winning the game. This cooperation increases solidarity and everlasting friendships even outside the field of play.
