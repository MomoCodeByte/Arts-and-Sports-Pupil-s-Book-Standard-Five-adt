Basic netball rules

  1. 1. The game is played in four quarters of 15 minutes each. A 3-minutes break is given between the first and second quarters. Players are given a 5-minutes break after the second quarter ends, which marks half time. Between the third and fourth quarters, players rest for another 3 minutes.
  2. 2. Each team must have seven players on the court. A team may make substitutions with reserve players during breaks or in case of injuries.
  3. 3. A player is not allowed to run with the ball.
  4. 4. Scoring is only done within the goal circle by players playing the GS and GA positions.
  5. 5. A player may not hold the ball for more than three seconds without passing or scoring.

Netball skill-building strategies

To become a good netball player, it is important to ensure the following strategies:

  1. a. Practising regularly. A player should practice regularly to strengthen their playing skills, such as passing, receiving passes, and shooting. These exercises help improve the accuracy and speed of passing, as well as the ability to receive the ball without dropping it.
  2. b. Participating in trial matches at school or out of school: Participating in trial matches is an excellent way to learn by doing. These matches enhance the experience of playing as a team, help players understand each other’s tactics, and improve communication between them. It also helps to increase confidence and strengthen the ability to make quick decisions during the game.
