How to play football and netball

Football

Football is a two-team sport played with the aim of scoring goals in the opponents’ goal.

Each team has 11 players, including the goalkeeper.

The game requires speed, concentration, and teamwork.

The main objective is to score more goals than the opposing team.

To score a goal, the ball must cross the goal line.
