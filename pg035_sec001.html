Drawing a human being's head

To draw the shape of a human being's head, consider the following steps:

  1. i. Draw two overlapping circular shapes—one resembling a ball and the other shaped like a mango;
  2. ii. Divide the shape you have drawn in to three parts using lines;
  3. iii. Draw line to indicate the position of the ears, eyes, nose, and mouth; and
  4. iv. Then, draw the ears, eyes, nose, and mouth following the guidelines you created in step three. Figure 6 shows steps on how to use circular shapes to draw human being's head.
