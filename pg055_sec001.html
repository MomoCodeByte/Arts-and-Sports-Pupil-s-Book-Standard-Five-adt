(c) Stencil decoration

To decorate a pot, use a stencil with the patterns that you want. Use the appropriate colours for the clay. Place the stencil on the area you want to print the impression. Apply the colour using a sponge or brush by gently and precisely touching the open area on the stencil. Figure 7 shows a pot decorated using stencils.
