A cylinder The use of cylinder shapes in drawing is a basic skill in drawing. It is used as a basis for drawing various objects that have a similar shape and enables you to draw interesting figures such as human beings, animals, and landscapes. Steps to draw a cylinder To draw a cylinder, consider the following steps: (i) Draw a horizontal rectangle, divide each side at the centre by placing a dot, and draw an oval shape by following the placed dots as demonstrated in step 1 in Figure 3; (ii) Draw the vertical line downward from the left and the right dot as demonstrated in step 2; (iii) Repeat step one to draw the bottom oval shape as demonstrated in step 3; and (iv) Erase all unwanted lines to remain with a clear cylinder shape as demonstrated in step 4.
