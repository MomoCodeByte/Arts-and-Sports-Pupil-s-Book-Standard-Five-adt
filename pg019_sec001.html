Importance of dynamics in music

In music, particularly group singing, it is essential to have an understanding of musical dynamics in order to:

(a)

Enhance the beauty and appeal of the song. Vocal variations in intensity between soft and loud make the song more exciting.

(b)

Effectively convey the message and emotions of the song. Vocal rising and falling in a song create an emotion and help listeners understand the song thoroughly.

(c)

Add variety and avoid monotonous singing. Variation in vocal intensity makes the singing lively and interesting.
