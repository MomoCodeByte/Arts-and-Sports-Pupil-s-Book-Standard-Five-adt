Acting

Introduction

Acting is a powerful tool in the performing arts, capable of conveying important messages to audiences for the purpose of inspiring change in society. In this chapter, you will learn about the concept of drama and acting, and types of acting. The competencies developed will enable you to act skillfully while following the principles of acting.

Concept of drama and acting

Drama and acting are very important concepts in the performing arts as they play a major role in conveying messages to the intended audience. Therefore, developing an insight into these concepts is essential in building skills in the field of acting.

Drama is a type of performing art that aims to convey a message to a live audience. In a drama performance, both the audience and the actor are present in the same place at the same time. In other forms of performing arts, such as music and film, this simultaneous presence of the two groups does not occur, or it is not necessary.

There are two types of drama performed on stage. The first type is written scripts, and the second type is improvised drama. The scripted drama differs from improvised drama because it requires long time to study and memorise the lines; while improvised drama depends on creativity of the actors.
