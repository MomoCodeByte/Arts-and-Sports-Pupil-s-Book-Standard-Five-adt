(b) Painted decorations

To decorate a pot by painting, use a brush or stick to draw lines, flowers, geometric shapes, or any theme that you like. Use colours that are appropriate for the clay or use other coloured clay as a drawing medium. The clay in the pot should be slightly damp to make it easier to draw.
