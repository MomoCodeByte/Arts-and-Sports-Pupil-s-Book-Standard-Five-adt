In clay modelling and firing tasks, the following activities should be done: (a) Putting out the fire in the kiln; (b) Collecting ashes and burying them; (c) Cleaning leftovers and burying them; and (d) Filling up and levelling the holes from which clay soil was obtained and where the kiln was set.
