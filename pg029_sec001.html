Chapter Three

Drawing

Introduction

Drawing requires a good composition of the image being drawn in order to convey the intended message. In this chapter, you will learn to identify the key elements of good composition. The competencies developed will enable you to draw a variety of well-composed images to express emotions and situations using a pencil.

Key elements in drawing images that express emotions and situations

Drawing images that express emotions and situations

Drawing images that express emotions and situations is an art that requires attention to the use of lines, shapes, balance, shadows, colours, cohesion, and texture. This kind of drawing requires a good arrangement of the image during sketching.

Drawing materials

There are some of the materials used in drawing images. These are pencil, sharpener, paper, eraser, table and chair, drawing board, and paper clip.

Shapes

Drawing involves how the artist creates and uses shapes to convey ideas, feelings, or stories through art. In the drawing, shapes are used to create structure, proportion, and balance in the image.
