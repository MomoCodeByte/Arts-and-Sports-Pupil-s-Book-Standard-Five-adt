23 Principles of using shapes in drawing various objects Using shapes in drawing is a basic method that helps create accurate and well- structured drawings. The following are important techniques to consider when using shapes in drawing: (i) Use circles to start drawing parts of circular shapes, such as a head, a wheel, or parts of flowers; (ii) Use rectangles to show parts of objects with long sides, such as buildings, doors, or books; (iii) Use four or three-sided shapes to create objects with triangular and rectangular proportions, such as windows or boxes; (iv) Draw several shapes together to create a complex drawing. For example, connect circles and rectangles to create the body of a human being, animal, bird, or car; (v) Observe the size of the shape and space within to show the proportion of the object you are drawing; (vi) Use sketch lines to get a map of the real image before drawing it, then draw the real image following those lines; and (vii) Add shadows to the shapes in order to acquire depth and realism of the image. Circular shape Drawing using a circular shape is very important in artwork. This is because the circle is used as a basic part to draw other shapes, such as a mouth, head, or wheel. The skills required for an artist are a steady hand in drawing lines and knowing the proportions of the circle you want to draw. Steps to draw a circular shape There are many ways to draw a circular shape. In drawing some of these shapes, it is important to consider the following steps: (i) Draw a square as demonstrated in step 1 in Figure 1; (ii) Divide at the centre the drawn square in each side by using a dot as demonstrated in step 2; (iii) Draw the circle by following the dots placed on each side of the square as demonstrated in step 3; and (iv) Erase the square to remain with only the circle as demonstrated in step 4.
