Principles of using shapes in drawing various objects

Using shapes in drawing is a basic method that helps create accurate and well-structured drawings.

The following are important techniques to consider when using shapes in drawing:

(i)

Use circles to start drawing parts of circular shapes, such as a head, a wheel, or parts of flowers;

(ii)

Use rectangles to show parts of objects with long sides, such as buildings, doors, or books;

(iii)

Use four or three-sided shapes to create objects with triangular and rectangular proportions, such as windows or boxes;

(iv)

Draw several shapes together to create a complex drawing.

For example, connect circles and rectangles to create the body of a human being, animal, bird, or car;

(v)

Observe the size of the shape and space within to show the proportion of the object you are drawing;

(vi)

Use sketch lines to get a map of the real image before drawing it, then draw the real image following those lines; and

(vii)

Add shadows to the shapes in order to acquire depth and realism of the image.

Circular shape

Drawing using a circular shape is very important in artwork.

This is because the circle is used as a basic part to draw other shapes, such as a mouth, head, or wheel.

The skills required for an artist are a steady hand in drawing lines and knowing the proportions of the circle you want to draw.
