Vocabulary

Shadow:
The darkened area created when an object blocks light from reaching a surface
Balance:
The harmony or proportion of various elements in a piece of art to create a sense of calm, order, and visual appeal
Unity:
A principle that refers to the balance and good connection between different components in a drawing to make it appear as one complete work
Texture:
The degree of roughness or softness at a surface
