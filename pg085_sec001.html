Often, these games are played using simple and local materials, and their main features is the close interaction between the participants. One of the traditional games is tug-of-war. Tug-of-war Tug of war is one of the traditional games played by people of all ages. It is a competitive game that requires strength, teamwork, technique and endurance. Although it is an ancient game, it is still very popular in various communities, especially in school competitions, social celebrations, and in international games. Historically, this game is associated with many cultures around the world. In East Africa, this game has been part of the culture of many communities, associated with physical exercise, entertainment, and festive celebrations to date. The game is popular in school sports' competitions and cultural concerts. Game equipment In playing this game, various equipment are used such as a rope, whistle, lime and flagmaker. Rules and regulations of the game 1. Number of players: (a) The teams should have an equal number of players, usually between 3 and 8 people, but the number may vary depending on the competition. (b) Players should be in good health and physical strength to participate effectively. 2. Game structure: (a) A long rope with a centre mark is used. (b) A centre line is drawn on the ground to indicate the area beyond which either team loses. (c) Each team holds its side of the rope tightly and lines up in a neat formation, as shown in Figure 9.
