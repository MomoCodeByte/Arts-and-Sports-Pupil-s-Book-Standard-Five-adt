Activity 9
  1. In pairs, practise a tug-of-war game.
  2. In teams, play a tug-of-war game.

Benefits of tug-of-war

  1. Help strengthen the muscles of the arms, legs, back and shoulders.
  2. It enhances cooperation and strategy-setting skills as a team.
  3. Increases the spirit of competing fairly and enjoying victory wisely.
  4. It is a good entertainment for children and adults.
  5. It teaches patience and discipline.
Exercise 2
  1. Imagine you are on a tug-of-war team. Which position of the team would you like to be (front, middle, or back)? Why?
  2. Beside strength, what other things are needed to win a tug-of-war?
  3. What are the potential dangers of playing tug-of-war?
  4. Outline the precautionary measures of tug-of-war?
  5. Why is it important for a team to pull the rope together instead of each person pulling separately?
  6. Which team is declared the winner in tug-of-war?
