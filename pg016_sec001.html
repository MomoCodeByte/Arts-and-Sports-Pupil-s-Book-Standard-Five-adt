Challenges of mime Although it is a very important form of acting, mime faces several challenges, including: (a) It is difficult for actors to convey a message to the audience if they do not have sufficient skill; (b) Mime requires a long time of practise and rehearsal to convey a message to the audience; (c) Limited understanding of the audience, as not everyone has the ability to interpret performances that do not use words to convey the message, making some messages not easily understood; Despite these challenges, mime as a form of acting remains an important art form that requires patience, creativity and dedication to make it more engaging and effective. Activity 4 Use reliable online sources to find a short mime film, then briefly analyse the themes of the performance. Activity 5 (a) In a group of not more than eight people, create a short play based on one of the following themes: the effects of corruption; the benefits of tax education; the dangers of infectious diseases; gender-based violence; environmental mismanagement; or the consequences of laziness. (b) Perform the mime play you have created. (c) Conduct a discussion about the benefits and challenges of mime.
