Challenges of mime

Although it is a very important form of acting, mime faces several challenges, including:

(a)

It is difficult for actors to convey a message to the audience if they do not have sufficient skill;

(b)

Mime requires a long time of practise and rehearsal to convey a message to the audience;

(c)

Limited understanding of the audience, as not everyone has the ability to interpret performances that do not use words to convey the message, making some messages not easily understood;

Despite these challenges, mime as a form of acting remains an important art form that requires patience, creativity and dedication to make it more engaging and effective.
