(b) Practise shooting from different angles around the goal. (e) Defending Defending is the act of preventing the opposing team from passing the ball or scoring. Defensive players such as the goalkeeper (GK) and goal defender (GD) must be quick and able to read the game in order to stop the opposing team’s attackers. The main types of defending in netball are: (i) Man-to-man defence: A player follows an opponent and attempts to block a pass or shot. (ii) Zonal defence: Defensive players stay in specific areas to ensure there is no space for the opponents.
