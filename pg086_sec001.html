3. Method of determining the winner:

(a)

The team that can pull the rope and move the opposing team across the centre line is declared the winner.

(b)

Sometimes, the game is played in more than one round, and the team that wins the most rounds is awarded the overall winner.

Strategies for winning the game

(a)

Teamwork. Working together and planning a pull-up strategy instead of each person pulling independently.

(b)

Organising players in good order.

Some players are strategically placed at the front, middle, and back to provide maximum support for the team.

For instance, the strongest players may be placed at the front, middle and back.

(c)

Holding the rope strongly and tightly.

Players should hold the rope strongly and tightly and keep their hands close to their bodies for stability.

(d)

Using one back leg as the main force.

Players should place one foot behind to gain good footing and pulling power, as shown in Figure 10.

(e)

Preventing slipping.

Wearing shoes that provide good traction on the ground helps prevent slipping.
