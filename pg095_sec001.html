Why is defending important in football? Basic laws of football There are 17 laws of football. At this stage, you will learn the following five laws: 1. An official football match is played for 90 minutes, 45 minutes per half paced and a 15-minute half-time break. 2. In an official match, the number of players for each team is 11 players, including the goalkeeper. 3. A goal is scored if the ball completely crosses the goal line. 4. Offside occurs when a player receives the ball in front of the opposing team’s defenders without another player of the opposing team other than the goalkeeper. 5. A foul happens if a player wrongly plays against an opponent. Activity 7 (a) Play football with your friends for 30 minutes, focusing on the skills you have learned.
