1. (d)

    Prepare a plaster mixture by mixing sand, cement, and a small amount of water;

  2. (e)

    Measure and cut the wire mesh to fit both sides of the flowerpot;

  3. (f)

    Apply a 2cm thick layer of plaster to the flowerpot, and use a piece of rope to divide the plaster into two halves. Once the first layer is set, place the wire mesh onto the soft plaster on both sides of the pot. Then, add a second layer of plaster to fully cover the wire mesh on both sides, ensuring the rope remains exposed;

  4. (g)

    Smooth the edges of both sides of the pot properly;

  5. (h)

    Put the pot in the shade to allow it to dry, as seen in Figure 3.
