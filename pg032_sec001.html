Cube and rectangular cuboid A cube and a rectangular cuboid are similar shapes, except that a cube is shorter and a rectangular cuboid is longer. A cube shape has the appearance of a box with all four sides of the same length, while a rectangular cuboid has the appearance of a box with two shorter sides of the same length and another two longer sides of the same length. These shapes can be used as the basis for drawing different objects, like buildings.
