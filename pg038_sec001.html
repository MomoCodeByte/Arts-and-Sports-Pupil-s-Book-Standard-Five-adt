Drawing a human being in different postures

People can be drawn in different positions, such as sitting, sleeping, walking, jumping, and so on. The position of the body parts describes the postures of a human being, whether sitting, lying, walking, running, or jumping. For example, if you want to draw a human being lying down, you will make sure that their arms, legs, chest, and head are drawn in a horizontal direction. If you want to draw a human being walking or running, you will draw the arms, legs, chest, and head positioned between the vertical and horizontal direction, that is, between horizontal and vertical.
