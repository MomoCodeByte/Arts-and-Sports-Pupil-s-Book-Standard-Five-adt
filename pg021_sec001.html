Voice balancing

Voice balancing involves maintaining equal volume levels in singing. It is crucial in singing by involving multiple voices at the same time to achieve a harmonious blend. The key factors to ensure voice balancing in singing include:

  1. (a)

    Singing a song while listening to other voices. Each singer should listen to the voices of others and sing in balance.

  2. (b)

    No voice should overlap with another without arrangement.

  3. (c)

    The voices should be at equal levels of volume.

  4. (d)

    All voices should be well-balanced and heard clearly without overlapping.

  5. (e)

    Practice proper breathing techniques and vocal control to improve singing and voice balance.
