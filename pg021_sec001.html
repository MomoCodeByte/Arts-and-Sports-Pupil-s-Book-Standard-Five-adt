Solfa singing This is a method of singing melodies using specific syllables that represent musical pitches instead of the actual lyrics of a song. These syllables are known as solfa. They are: do, re, mi, fa, so, la, ti. This method makes it easier to learn accurate note singing and helps improve the ability to read and write music. Figure 1 presents pupils singing using solfa.
