(d)  Planks

Planks strengthen the abdominal and back muscles, helping players to be stable when running or jumping.

How to do planks:

  1. Lie face down, supporting yourself on your hands and toes;
  2. Keep your body upright; do not let your waist dip or rise; and
  3. Hold this position for 30 to 60 seconds, then relax and repeat 3 times, by following steps (i-ii) as shown in Figure 4.
