How to do planks: (i) Lie face down, supporting yourself on your hands and toes; (ii) Keep your body upright; do not let your waist dip or rise; and (iii) Hold this position for 30 to 60 seconds, then relax and repeat 3 times, by following steps (i-ii) as shown in Figure 4.
