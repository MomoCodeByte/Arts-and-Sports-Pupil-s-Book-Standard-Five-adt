It is an important skill in netball, where only Goal Shooters (GS) and Goal Attackers (GA) are allowed to shoot. To shoot effectively, the player should consider the following: (i) Holding the ball correctly. The player should hold the ball with one or two hands, depending on their distance from the net. The fingers should be open to rest the ball on the palms for good control. (ii) Bending the knees slightly. The player should bend the knees slightly and then push the body up to gain power for the shot and increase the accuracy of the ball towards the net. (iii) Aiming the net. The player’s eyes should be at the top of the net to ensure the ball is directed correctly. (iv) Releasing the ball.
