(d) Shooting Shooting is the action of accurately throwing the ball so that it enters the opponent’s goal, as shown in Figure 15. It is an important skill in netball, where only Goal Shooters (GS) and Goal Attackers (GA) are allowed to shoot. To shoot effectively, the player should consider the following: (i) Holding the ball correctly. The player should hold the ball with one or two hands, depending on their distance from the net. The fingers should be open to rest the ball on the palms for good control. (ii) Bending the knees slightly. The player should bend the knees slightly and then push the body up to gain power for the shot and increase the accuracy of the ball towards the net. (iii) Aiming the net. The player’s eyes should be at the top of the net to ensure the ball is directed correctly. (iv) Releasing the ball. The ball should be released with a backspin so that it enters the net easily and reduces the possibility of going out. The player should bend the knees a little. Then the player should push the body up. This gives more power to the shot and helps the ball go more accurately to the net. (iii) Aim at the net. The player should look at the top of the net. This helps the ball go in the right direction. (iv) Release the ball. The player should release the ball with a backspin.
