Players can defend in different ways, such as: (i) Man-to-man defending: A player follows an opponent closely to prevent them from receiving the ball or shooting the ball. (ii) Zonal defending: Players stay in specific areas of the field to ensure that there are no opportunities for the opposing team to penetrate.
