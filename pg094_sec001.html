(d) Shooting

Shooting refers to kicking the ball towards the opponent’s goal with the aim of scoring a goal. To shoot effectively, a player must:

  1. 1. Kick the ball with power and accuracy, aiming for the angles of the goal to make it difficult for the goalkeeper to catch it.
  2. 2. Use different parts of the foot (ankle for an accurate shot, upper leg for a powerful shot).
  3. 3. Position the body in the right direction to get the ball towards the right direction.
