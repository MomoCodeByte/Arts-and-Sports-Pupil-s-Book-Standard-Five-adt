Steps to create a pot using a mould The following are the steps for making a pot using a mould: (a) Begin by laying a light nylon sheet inside the mould to prevent the clay from sticking to it; (b) Press the clay into the mould to form a layer, similar to coconut flesh in its husk, aiming for a thickness of about 2 cm; (c) Smooth out the surface of the clay for evenness; (d) Create scratch marks along the edges of the clay; this helps to strengthen the bond when the pieces are joined later; (e) Allow some time for the malleable clay to set so that it does not get dented; ART & SPORTS 5 a.indd 46 ART & SPORTS 5 a.indd 46 17/09/2025 10:07:08 17/09/2025 10:07:08 F O R O N L I N E R E A D I N G O N L Y
