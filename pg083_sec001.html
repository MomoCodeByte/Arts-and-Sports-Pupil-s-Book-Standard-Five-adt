(b)  Shuttle runs

This is a high-intensity exercise that involves sprinting from one point to another point and going back to the starting point. It helps to improve the speed and endurance of the player.

How to do shuttle runs:

(c)  Broad jumps

This exercise increases jumping power and speed. This is important in playing various sports, such as netball and football.

How to do broad jumps:

  1. i. Stand upright, then bend your knees slightly;
  2. ii. Jump forward with force and land on both feet; and
  3. iii. Repeat 5 to 10 times for 3 sets, by following steps (i-ii) as shown in Figure 7.
