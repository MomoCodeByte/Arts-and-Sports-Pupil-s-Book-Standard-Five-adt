This weight can be physical, felt when carrying something, or visual, perceived by looking at objects. In drawing, balance refers to the visual weight perceived through sight. An image needs to have a balance; otherwise, it may make the viewer feel a sense of imbalance and instability. For example, Figure 21 shows an unbalanced image where the artist places many objects (a tree, a rock, and a hill) on the left side, making the image appear tilted toward that side.
